Presets stay now-anchored: the panel's strip is a zoom on the recent horizon while the past lives in the list. Incident-centered windows are deferred as scope and interaction, not truth — a past window renders truthfully from its own explicitly-bounded document, and the existing vouching machinery (history-document coverage plus the status-vouched tail) already extends to it; the deferral is whether a past window is ever wanted on a strip, which the eye pass judges.
